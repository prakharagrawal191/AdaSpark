# Data Availability

The datasets generated and/or analysed during the current study are not publicly available due to a pending archival deposit — the raw observation records carry identifying machine metadata that are withheld during review, and the versioned archive receives its persistent identifier only at deposit (DEC-045) — but are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request. The analysis code, frozen policies, decision log and derived analysis artifacts are kept with SHA-256 checksums in the project repository, so every reported figure can be re-derived from the archived records.
