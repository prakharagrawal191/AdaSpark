# Data Availability

The datasets generated and/or analysed during the current study are available in two documented tiers: **committed to the repository** — the `results/` manifests and evaluation analyses (including `results/evaluation/*.json`), the EXP-002 raw runs, and the preprocessed observation record — and **held and deposit-pending under DEC-045 D1–D11** — the raw X-family confirmation observations (`results/experiments/x*`), which the repository's `results/**` ignore rule excludes from version control (held in the working tree, not yet archived). The preprocessed observation record (`results/experiments/exp-009/ext/observations_ext.jsonl`, 4.8 MB, 4,842 rows) is committed, and per-file SHA-256 checksums and recorded file times are stored alongside. The 4,842 raw observation records and the complete result tree carry identifying machine metadata and are withheld during review; they are packaged as a versioned archive and will be deposited in an archival repository with a DOI at camera-ready. All data generated or analysed during this study that can be shared without de-anonymization risk are included in the supplementary information files or covered by that DEC-045 deposit plan. Frozen trained policies (`models/policies/`) and the analysis code (`scripts/`) are included for full reproducibility of every derived figure. Provenance notes: `docs/research/CLAIM_EXPERIMENT_MAP.md` §Provenance notes (DEC-052 D2).
