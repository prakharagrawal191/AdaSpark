# 15. Results and Findings

**Sensitivity (EXP-002, Figure 21 context):** configuration choice moves median execution time by ≥10% on all four tested families — the feasibility gate passes and the learning problem is real. **Training (EXP-004, Figure 23):** three seeds ran 84/49/42 episodes to ε = 0.05/0.081/0.116 under the 500-guard (232 spent at gate time; 483 total), with zero failed submitted configurations — but cross-seed greedy agreement reached only 0.20 against the 0.70 threshold, so no single policy was promoted and all three replicates were evaluated as separate arms.

**Main comparison — EXP-005 (descriptive) and EXP-013 (confirmatory; Figures 21 and 24, Table 11):** EXP-005 executed 245/245 runs (195 usable) across 7 frozen instances × 7 arms; on its six common cells the median-of-5 sums were B0 142.38, B1 30.32, B4 38.34 and RL-s0 38.15 s, read at the time as "RL matches random search but not static tuning", with no inferential test run. Its arms ran in a fixed instance-then-arm queue, and arms executing the identical configuration differed by up to 2.16× (`F1_agg|large|s3`), so that ordering was confounded with queue position. EXP-013 re-tested it under the pre-registered design of DEC-053: 900 queue entries over 42 frozen TEST cells in randomized interleaved blocks, 790 usable executions, 0 charged to SC6. **H2 is accepted** for every RL arm: the learned policy needs 0.249× the default's time (geometric-mean ratio, Equation 12; 95% CI 0.212–0.295) and is ≥10% faster with cell-level significance on 36 of 37 decidable cells (one-sided exact Wilcoxon, Holm-adjusted p = 4.4×10⁻¹¹). **H3 is rejected:** against the tuned static configuration B1 — to which the rule heuristic B3 and the random search B4 also resolve on every TEST cell — RL is 1.22× slower (CI 1.13–1.32), slower beyond the noise band on 16 cells (all 15 F4_ski cells and `F5_mixed|medium`) and faster on one (`F3_rdd|small`, 0.75×). The deficit is structural rather than noisy: on F1_agg and F2_join the policy selects the static configuration itself (ratio 1.01), on F4_ski its parallelism-2 choice is 1.60× slower on every cell, and on F5_mixed it is within noise (1.06). The A/A control explains EXP-005's ordering: B4 and B1 execute the same configuration and differ by a median 2.0% (maximum 9.7%; all 38 cells inside the 11.89% band; two-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.19), so EXP-005's 2.16× spreads were queue position, not configuration, and "RL ≈ random search" was an artifact — random search converged to the static configuration, and RL trails both alike (RL/B4 1.21, CI 1.11–1.31; the pre-registered verdict is INCONCLUSIVE only because that interval's lower end touches the band edge).

**Structural failures and secondary results (EXP-013).** All four probed `F3_rdd|large` cells (seeds 0, 1, 2, 4) failed with the same Windows sort/spill file lock as seed 3, confirming that failure as structural. A new one appeared on `F3_rdd|medium|s4`: the default and the RL policy's rdd_sort choice (`G-p4-sp128`) failed 5/5 while the validation-tuned static configurations completed every run; the pre-registered halt rule stopped the campaign for diagnosis, and an operator-signed amendment (DEC-055) let the cell finish as frozen. Re-measured side by side, AQE-on (B0′) was faster than B0 beyond noise on none of six cells — X6's −14.24% on `F2_join|large` became +1.8% — so that cross-day lead is retracted. Fresh medians matched EXP-005 within ±5% for 18 of 39 cell-arms, the misses concentrating where EXP-005's medians had been inflated by queue position, and X10's "medium reproduces, small drifts" pattern did not recur. Lag-1 autocorrelation in queue order was +0.86, reproducing the regime-structured drift of EXP-009, and an exploratory check found no carry-over from heavy default-configuration runs (8.1% versus 10.0% slow runs after versus not after B0).

**Generalization (EXP-006):** partial — 61 usable of 125 planned with heavy structural failures on unseen cells, so SC5 is not evaluable. **Overhead (EXP-009, Figure 22):** 4,842 runs (3,228 usable) over ~57.4 wall-clock hours; six of seven cells PASS the 5% budget by whole-interval position, with sysmon point estimates within ±0.2%. The pre-registered √n prediction failed on all seven cells (observed/predicted 0.18×–7.70×) because repetitions are serially correlated episodes, not independent draws.

**Robustness re-analysis (R1–R8, new):** seed choice moves half-widths ≤0.1pp and BCa matches percentile CIs — the intervals are seed- and method-stable. But block bootstraps that respect serial dependence widen half-widths up to ~26pp and flip two of six PASS verdicts to INCONCLUSIVE; ACF stays +0.3…+0.7 at lag 3; CUSUM level shifts (up to ±38%) land at the same repetition in all three interleaved arms — host drift, not overhead. The bimodality coefficient for the non-converging cell is 0.485 (below the 0.555 heuristic), so its distribution is reported as skewed-with-secondary-mass rather than bimodal. A "first PASS anywhere" stopping rule would have halted that cell at n = 29 — it never converged — quantifying the selection hazard. Supporting studies complete the picture. EXP-003 baseline selection ran 96 validation observations (12 configs × 8 cells × 1 rep) to freeze B1 (`G-p8-sp16`) and per-family B2 assignments; the planned 120-run formal comparison was separately authorized after the register gap (estimate ~30 vs required 216) was recorded as a plan defect. EXP-007 state ablations (A1: 35+35 episodes; A2: 35+21) found 0/4 and 0/2 cross-seed greedy agreement with single-episode evidence on key states — no-evidence zones reported, not smoothed over. EXP-008's derived ablation recomputed 48 evidence pairs at zero execution cost. Training density explains the disagreement: 16.8/9.8/8.4 episodes per evidence-bearing state per seed, 41 of 60 state-action cells ever visited (68.3%), and the single unanimous state resting on one episode per seed executed by no seed — the M8 failure is a measurement-density fact, and the report treats it as such. The measured per-execution reward noise floor is 0.069 (largest repeat-execution range on an identical cell-action-config), and the SC6 ledger closes at 483/500: 232 training baseline + 84 B4 search + 20 EXP-001 + 126 EXP-007 + 21 smoke, with 17 remaining (488/500 combined — 12 remaining — once the five disclosed demo TRAIN runs of EXP-012 are added).

**Confirmation pilots (X-family, own register lines, 0 charged to SC6; DEC-047–051):** the heuristic collapse is confirmed empirically — B3 resolved byte-identically to B1 on 7/7 executed validation cells (X1), while the sampler sweep (X2, n=1, +9.4/−7.3/+1.8% across 0.5/1/2 Hz) and the event-log confound pair (X3, −3.9%, inside the 11.89% noise band) stay descriptive and bounded, changing no verdict. The AQE-on arm B0′ (X6, 35 runs, 30 usable) is **mixed**: `F2_join|large` −14.24% outside noise in favor of B0′, the other five decidable cells inside noise or reversed, `F3_rdd|large` incomplete — side-by-side evidence only, pooling with RL arms forbidden — a cross-day comparison that EXP-013's interleaved re-test did not reproduce. The F2 lead rematch (X8) **falsified its own hypothesis**: a fresh same-day run gave B1 2.964 s vs RL-s1 2.947 s (−0.57%), so neither the EXP-005 −8.6% lead (vs B4, in-noise, different baseline) nor the cross-day −24.7% implied by an earlier figure description (unsourced, dropped) was reproduced — drift, not a stable lead. The NYC-Taxi pilot (X9, 20 runs) established transfer **feasibility** — tuned −78.05%/−69.82% vs B0 with the frozen RL-s0 greedy action identical to the tuned configuration, verified from the artifact (30 planned runs → 20 executed plus identity, substitution disclosed) — not a generality verdict; SC5 remains NOT EVALUABLE. The SC7 pilot (X10, 20 runs) returned **PARTIAL 2/4**: medium arms reproduce within ±5% (+2.71%/+4.74%), small arms miss it (+6.82%/+11.73%), so reproducibility claims stay pilot-scoped while the full 60-run EXP-011 remains unauthorized (gap G-01). The live demo rehearsal (EXP-012) ran 5/5 usable TRAIN-split executions on its own line — B0 4.699 s slowest, B3 and the frozen policy clustering near 1.4–1.8 s, episode 1 acting on `G-p8-sp32` (the pre-registered `G-p8-sp16` identity parenthetical did not hold and is reported as-is).
