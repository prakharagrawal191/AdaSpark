# 21. Future Research Directions

Figure 30 orders the agenda by evidence readiness. **Nearest (pre-designed):** the once-nearest items — the scoped AQE-on arm (EXP-005b, 35 runs, own ledger) and the validation-scoped B3 heuristic, sampler and event-log confirmations — have since been executed as X6 and X1–X3 under DEC-047/048 (X6's lead later retracted by EXP-013); what remains nearest is a state whose size bins match the actual data range (today every input falls in one bin), root-causing the Windows sort/spill file lock behind every `F3_rdd` failure, and the `F3_rdd|medium` calibration backfill (gap G-05, needs its own TRAIN decision). **Medium term:** multi-agent RL over partitioned configuration subspaces (one agent per axis, coordinated through the reward), federated optimization across teams sharing Q0 calibrations without sharing data, and autonomous cluster-scale validation of the single-node findings — each requiring its own budget line and pre-registration. **Longer term** (Figure 31): LLM-assisted optimization (language models proposing configurations the RL loop adjudicates, keeping measurement as the authority), cognitive data engineering (policies that explain themselves in operator language, grounded in the traceability layer of Section X), and dependence-aware stopping rules built from the CUSUM/ACF evidence of Section XV — converting this report's retrospective cost finding into a prospective method. Every direction inherits the guardrails: bounded budgets, sealed evaluation, frozen analysis code, and negative results published.

The literature supplies concrete starting points. Multi-agent deep RL, including agents that learn to communicate, is surveyed in depth [134][135], and cooperative multi-task MARL is a natural fit for coordinating a partition agent with a parallelism agent [136]. Federated learning on resource-constrained devices [137] and federated Q-learning for industrial resource allocation [138] show how calibration knowledge could be shared without sharing data, at the price of new attack surfaces [113]. LLM-based agents already tune database knobs [139], and DB-GPT outlines LLM-driven data management [140]; in AdaSpark's design such a model would propose configurations while measurement stays the authority. Energy-aware objectives, now reviewed for data-centre RL [133], would extend the reward beyond execution time.

Sequencing matters because budgets are finite (17 executions remain under the training cap): the validation-scoped confirmations came first and are done (X1–X3, DEC-048); the F3 calibration backfill remains. The TEST-crossing work proceeded under explicit authorization decisions — AQE-on arm X6 (DEC-047), SC7 pilot X10 (DEC-049) and EXP-013 (DEC-053/055) — since the seal is the project's most valuable methodological asset; the F2 rematch X8 crossed it outside its recorded scope, disclosed in DEC-052. External validity has one feasibility pilot (X9 Taxi, DEC-050) but its full form — a second public family, the 60-run reproducibility study — comes last because it is the most expensive and the least constrained by current gaps: it extends the claims rather than securing them. This ordering ensures that if resources run out, what remains unfinished is clearly marked future work rather than quietly weakened evidence.
